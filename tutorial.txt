Goal:
Deliver all required resources to the Sink nodes before time runs out.

Nodes:
    Source (Circle): Produces a single resource (e.g., RED, BLUE, YELLOW).
    Processor (Square): Combines two resources to produce a new one (e.g., RED + BLUE → PURPLE).
    Sink (Octagon): Demands specific resource(s). Providing incorrect resources causes an error.

Connecting Nodes:
    Clicking on a node (Sources or Processors) starts the connection.
    Click on the target node’s (Processors or Sinks) to complete the pipe.
    Each Source/Processor can have two outgoing pipes; each Sink can accept two resource types.

Simulation Control:
    Start or Stop flow with the “Start Sim / Stop Sim” button or pressing "E"
    Stop it at any time to adjust pipes or if an error occurs.
    Reset Pipes (R) clears all pipes (useful after errors).
    Be Careful, you clear all the pipes at once so choose your connections carefully.

Color Mixing & Win Condition:
    Color Recipes: Use Processors to combine source colors (e.g., RED + YELLOW = ORANGE).
    Sink Demands: A Sink might need one or two distinct resources. Fulfill all demands to win.
    Scoring: Faster completion + fewer pipes = higher score.
