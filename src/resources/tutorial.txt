How to Play:

Goal:
Satisfy all sink demands before time runs out.

Node Types:
• SOURCE (CIRCLES): Produces a resource.
• PROCESSOR (SQUARES): Combines inputs to produce a new resource.
• SINK (OCTAGONS): Requires a specific resource.

Instructions:

Connect Nodes:
    Click a node’s output connector (for sources or processors) then click a valid target’s input connector (processor or sink).

Start Simulation:
    Press “Start Sim” to begin resource flow.

Monitor & Control:
    – If a sink gets the wrong resource, stop the sim and use “Reset Pipes (R)” to rebuild.
    – You can always manually stop the sim.

Win the Level:
    When all sinks are satisfied, you clear the level and earn points based on time remaining and pipeline efficiency.

Faster Time + Fewer Pipes = Higher Score!
